My Future Goals

Here's my vision for the next 5 years and beyond.

Year 1 (2024-2025)

Complete High School Strongly

My immediate goal is to finish my current school year with excellent grades. I'm focusing on improving my math and science skills, as these are areas I want to excel in for my future career.

Specific Goal: Achieve a GPA of 3.8 or higher and participate in at least one academic competition.

Year 2 (2025-2026)

College Preparation

During my junior year, I'll be focusing on college preparation. This includes taking standardized tests, researching potential schools, and building a strong application portfolio.

Specific Goal: Score in the top 20% on SAT/ACT exams and visit at least 5 college campuses.

Year 3 (2026-2027)

College Applications & Senior Year

My senior year will be dedicated to finalizing my college applications and making the most of my last year in high school. I'll also be looking for scholarship opportunities.

Specific Goal: Apply to at least 8 colleges and secure scholarships totaling at least $10,000.

Year 4 (2027-2028)

First Year of College

I plan to attend college and pursue a degree in Computer Science or a related field. My first year will be about adjusting to college life, making new friends, and exploring different academic paths.

Specific Goal: Maintain a GPA of 3.5 or higher and join at least 2 student organizations.

Year 5 (2028-2029)

College Sophomore Year

By my second year of college, I hope to have declared my major and started taking more specialized courses. I also want to gain practical experience through internships or part-time jobs.

Specific Goal: Complete an internship in my chosen field and develop a professional portfolio.

My 5-Year Vision

In five years, I see myself as a confident, knowledgeable college student with a clear career path. I hope to have gained valuable experience through internships, built a strong professional network, and developed skills that will help me succeed in my chosen field. My ultimate goal is to graduate with honors and be well-prepared for the next chapter of my life, whether that's entering the workforce or pursuing further education.
