About Me

My Story

I am a dedicated high school teacher at Grossmont High School in San Diego County, with 27 years of teaching experience across Arizona, Texas, and California. I specialize in Business, Finance, and Technology classes, and I am passionate about Applied Learning and Project Based Learning methodologies that help students engage with real-world challenges.

A family man at heart, I have a wonderful wife, two sons, and a loyal dog. Beyond teaching, I have a deep passion for travel, exploring historical sites and ruins, admiring architecture both old and new, and experiencing diverse cultures and cuisines. I average about 40 nights of international travel and 20 nights of domestic travel each year, and while I prefer the adventure of traveling, I am starting to appreciate the relaxation of vacationing more as I get older.

My interests span Business, Technology, AI, Space, and History. I enjoy fishing, hiking, camping, swimming, playing poker, and being social. Professionally, I am active in the stock markets, a founder of an edtech startup, and I present AI professional development sessions for educators.
